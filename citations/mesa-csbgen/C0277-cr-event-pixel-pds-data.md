Line 490 of `cr.xml` in Mesa's Rogue csbgen names `ROGUE_CR_EVENT_PIXEL_PDS_DATA`. It backs the
glossary register `CR_EVENT_PIXEL_PDS_DATA`: data size of the pixel-event PDS program in 16-byte
rows (Rogue: its data address).
