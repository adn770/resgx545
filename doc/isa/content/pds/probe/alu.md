### Bitwise ops, `add` and `sub`

*Measured on the hardware (2026-10-24).* `xor`, `nor`, `nand`, `and` and `or` are as named, on 32
bits (`pds-alu-bitwise`), and `add` and `sub` are modular with `dst = src - ds1[n]`
(`pds-alu-add-sub`)
**[HW]**.
