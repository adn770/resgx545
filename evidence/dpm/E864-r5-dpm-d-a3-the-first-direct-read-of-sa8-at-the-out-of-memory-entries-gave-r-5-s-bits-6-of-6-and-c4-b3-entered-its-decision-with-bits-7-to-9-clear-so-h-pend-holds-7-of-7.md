**What ran.** R-5 read the guard of the firmware's raise from its instructions (3d.md §6.4, [RE,
static]): the resume sets `sa8` bits 7 and 8 when the global page count it reads is above dword 36,
and the next out-of-memory entry raises when bit 7 is set, bit 9 clear and the count is above dword
36 again. No `sa8` had been read in a DPM job. H-PEND (the resume read a count one page below the
timeline's sample after a grow, E861) predicts c4-b3's decision entry with bits 7 to 9 clear and
`sa9` bit 8 clear at the end of the partial render; H-BLOCK by a skipped resume predicts `sa9` bit 8
set there. Stage d-a3 read both through our own task, armed before each job.

**Results [HW, frozen verdict].** `out/a2/score-d-a3.txt`: "H-BITS (R-5's sa8 semantics, first
direct read) (6 of 6 rows decided) | H-PEND on the rungs: 7 held, 0 FAILED, 0 cannot tell of 7 |
H-BLOCK on the rungs: 3 held, 4 FAILED, 0 cannot tell of 7".

**Read after the run [HW, post hoc].** In the four jobs that grew, the first out-of-memory rung
had `pa1` bit 21 and the one after it `pa1` bit 20 with `sa8` bit 22 set, and `sa8` bit 19 then
stayed set through the first partial render and the decision entry; in the four that did not grow
the first out-of-memory rung (bit 21) was followed directly by the pass with `pa1` bit 10 that
comes before the end of render in every job. That is 3d.md's
list-kind branch as read from the instructions: the grow on the bit-21 entry sets bit 22 and
restarts, and the bit-20 entry that follows writes the saved list back [infer].

**What it shows [infer].** The guard is as R-5 read it, now on the hardware: bits 7 and 8 come from
the resume and gate the raise, bit 9 stays clear, and a raised entry is followed by one with bit 9
set as well. c4-b3's resume did not set bits 7 and 8 although the timeline's last in-stretch sample
read 2 global pages in some repeats (E860): the count the resume compared was 1, not above dword
36 (3 at the abort, less 2). Nothing skips the resume after a grow (`sa9` bit 8 clear, the S3 cell
raised). So R-5's miss is closed: the guard was right, the count we estimated for it was a page
high.

**What it does not show.** Why the page is pending at the resume only after a grow; the operand
order of `isub16` stays [infer] (no cell with the two counts equal and non-zero was read). One
scene, one target, eight jobs; the task logs passes of the four slots only, not the exit paths.
