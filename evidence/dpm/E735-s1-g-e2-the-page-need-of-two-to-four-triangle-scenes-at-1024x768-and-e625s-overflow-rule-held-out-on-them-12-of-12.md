**What ran.** g-e2a measured U at P = 0x422 for six scenes (2, 3, 4 triangles apart: moves (0,288),
(-64,-240), (-768,-96); stacked: (16,0), (32,0), (48,0)), with E705's one triangle (U = 19) and
E627's two-triangle st-two-f at 0x38 (overflow) as controls. No formula for U was frozen (the
stream-byte model HU1 was not computed): the scorer reports U and its residual against an
empirical coverage proxy and passes nothing. g-e2b, generated from those U at the pause and frozen
before it ran, put each scene at P = U + 0x21 (overflow predicted) and U + 0x22 (completion
predicted), and two jobs of the four-triangle scene at P = 0x27 and 0x28 whose partial-render
counts split the (k, spacing) pairs E625 left open.

**Frozen verdicts.** g-e2a: U measured for 6 of 6 scenes (no hypothesis scored). g-e2b: E625's
overflow rule holds on 12 boundary jobs (held out).

**Results [HW].** Control U = 19; st-two-f overflowed at 0x38 as in E627. U (peak = walk in all
nine 0x422 jobs): 2a 29, 3a 35, 4a 37, 2s 19, 3s 22, 4s 22; the repeats of 4a and 4s and the
stopped run's u-1, u-2f and u-2a gave the same numbers. Every job printed K - 1 "geometry only, the
scene left open" lines. Boundary: overflow at U + 0x21 and completion at U + 0x22 in all six
scenes; knob proof (0x0624 = P - 0x22) in every job.

**What the boundary verdict covers [HW, read after the run].** In 10 of the 12 jobs the overflow or
completion is the K-triangle scene's. In b-2s-lo (thr0 0x12) the first kick overflowed (and the
closing render after it): one stacked triangle needs 19 pages, and the second added none. In b-4s-lo
(thr0 0x15) the third kick overflowed (U of three stacked = 22). The rule holds for the scene that
was open in both, not for the K-triangle scene. The g-e2b scorer did not check the open-kick lines
as g-e2a's does.

**The count jobs [HW].** At P = 0x27 and 0x28 the first three kicks each ended `scene overflowed`
after one partial render and the fourth completed alone (its moved triangle needs few pages): an
overflow ends the open scene and the next kick starts a new one. The three partial renders are
three one-triangle scenes at one each (E625's 1024x768 count). Every frozen (k, spacing) pair
predicted 1 or 2 for a four-triangle scene that did not form, so those predictions are void, not
refuted; the scorer printed no count result. Each overflowed kick's control block shows the raise
(dwords 37 and 38 = 0x10005 and 0x20, 0x10006 and 0x20), as E625's rule predicts at thr0 5 and 6.

**Post hoc (not held out).** [infer] Stacked copies 16 pixels apart add 0, 3 and 0 pages, apart
copies 10, 6 and 2: the need does not follow the triangle count. A formula (HU1) must be fitted
and frozen before a held-out run; the six U are fit data from now on.

**Not shown.** U's formula, E625's k and spacing (count jobs need P above one triangle's need with
several triangles still accumulating), and what the pages added by a second triangle hold.
