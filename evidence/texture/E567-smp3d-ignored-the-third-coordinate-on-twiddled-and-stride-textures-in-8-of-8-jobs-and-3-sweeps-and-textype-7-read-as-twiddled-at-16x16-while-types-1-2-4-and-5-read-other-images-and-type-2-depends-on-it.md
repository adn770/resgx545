**Results.** [HW] D7 per job and the pixel hashes of the rt* and ty* jobs. D7's "indicator" list (a
job that differs from its control) holds only the six bit-flip jobs; that those flips change an
image is not a sign that w acts (the same bits change smp2d images in E566, and the type 0 controls
show that w does not act). The job that does show w acting is TEXTYPE 2 (rt2-*), which D7 does not
list because it compares only the ind-, ty- and wsw- jobs with their 2D control. The follow-up
(gen_p1132.py --follow TAG T0 T1 T3, six cube directions and two slice sweeps) was generated by the
plan and was not run here.

**Predictions.** w3d=ignore held 8 of 8 fit jobs (TEXTYPE 0 and 6), and 4 of 11 explore jobs.

**What the simulator adds.** The simulator reads smp3d as smp2d only where it ran: TEXTYPE 0, 6
and 7, format 12, point filters, one level, the 16x16 twiddled and the 64x32 stride textures, with
the T0 and T3 words of these jobs; it refuses every other state, TEXTYPE 2 included, in that rule
itself.

**Not shown.** Why TEXTYPE 2 reads w (a cube or a volume are candidates; no job tested either), the
layout of types 1 to 5, smp3d with a LOD operand (does not assemble), bilinear or mipmapped smp3d,
types at other sizes than 16x16 (and 64x32 for 6).
