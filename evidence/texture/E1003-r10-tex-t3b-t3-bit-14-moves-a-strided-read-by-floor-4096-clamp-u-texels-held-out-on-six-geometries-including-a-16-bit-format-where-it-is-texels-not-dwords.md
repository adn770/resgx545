**What ran.** E566's tx_map form (scratch dword i = 0xD0000000 | i), strided textures (T1 type 6,
T3 0xA0804000 or bit 14 clear). Pairs: S 256 x W 64 x H 8 with u over [0, 1/4); S 16 x W 16 x H 64;
W = S = 64 with 4096 u = 64 x + 3/4 (floor and round part); W 32, S 64, u from -0.5 to 1.5, the
texture at dword 2048; u fixed at 5/16 with v = (y + 1/2) / 32; format 9 (u16) at S 64 on row 0.
Frozen per pixel: R-4096 (dwords), R-4096TX (texels), R-ROUND, H-U01 (S * S), H-UV (v for u) and
H-URAW (unclamped).

**Frozen verdict (as printed).** `H-E975 (replicate) (1 of 1 rows decided)`; `H-TEXMODEL (reference)
(6 of 6 rows decided)`; `H-U01 (heldout) is not supported: 3 held, 3 FAILED, 0 cannot tell of 6`;
`H-U01 (replicate) (1 of 1 rows decided)`; `H-URAW (heldout) is not supported: 0 held, 1 FAILED, 0
cannot tell of 1`; `H-UV (heldout) is not supported: 0 held, 1 FAILED, 0 cannot tell of 1`; `R-4096
(heldout) is not supported: 5 held, 1 FAILED, 0 cannot tell of 6`; `R-4096 (replicate) (1 of 1 rows
decided)`; `R-4096TX (heldout) (6 of 6 rows decided)`; `R-4096TX (replicate) (1 of 1 rows decided)`;
`R-ROUND (heldout) is not supported: 4 held, 2 FAILED, 0 cannot tell of 6`; `R-ROUND (replicate) (1
of 1 rows decided)`; both controls passed.

**Results [HW].** R-4096TX 2048 of 2048 on all six held-out rows and the replicate. R-ROUND 0 of
2048 on the off-grid row (the count is floored); H-U01 0 of 2048 at S 256 and S 16; H-URAW 1024 of
2048 on the clamp row (u clamps at both ends); H-UV 0 of 2048 (v does not enter); on the 16-bit
row even texels read dword x / 2 + 32 x + 16 (texels), not x / 2 + 64 x + 32 (dwords). Every
reference equals texmodel's image (the 16-bit one the half of dword x >> 1).

**What it shows.** [HW] T3 bit 14 on a strided texture moves the point-sampled read by floor(4096
clamp(u, 0, 1)) texels, whatever the stride, width or height and with v varying: E975's post hoc
reading held out, its unit texels (equal to dwords at 32 bits). sgx-sim models it (point sampled,
level 0, formats 9 and 12, no T3 offset, scale or conversion).

**What it does not show.** A twiddled texture (E566 faulted), filtered samples, other texel
sizes, mip levels; why 4096.
