**What runs.** The 64x64 triangle job writes state word 1 of its vertex stream as 0x0a022405 (bit 25
set, bit 12 clear). The sweep flips each bit of it and classifies the image against the unpoked job
(1062 triangle pixels of 4096, hash 0x99385a04ee01c585). The probe jobs set the word whole, with the
pixel program replaced by a copy of o0, and dump the parameter buffer pages and the TA data after
the render (`dump_render`); the dumps are read with `tools/usse-asm/pbdump.py`.

**Results.** Sweep, both runs: bit 12 (`^=0x1000`) gives an image with no triangle pixel, 4096
background pixels, 936 away from the baseline; bit 18 (`^=0x40000`) hangs the job; bit 25
(`^=0x2000000`) is not among the flips that change anything, so its image is the baseline. Bit 10
(`^=0x400`) removes the triangle too, as bits 11:9 at 0 do (E060).

Probes, both runs:

- bit 25 clear: the parameter buffer pages (4603904 bytes) and the TA data (24576 bytes) are byte
  for byte those of the default word, in the two runs; the object is 23 dwords and seven of the 64
  regions are non-empty (57 empty). The images of the probe job show the unwritten-register
  leftovers of E060: 3792 and 304 pixels for the default word and 3640 and 456 with bit 25 clear in
  the first run, 3807 and 289 for both in the follow-up run, so the pixel counts do not separate
  them;
- bit 12 set, with bit 25 set or clear: the image is background only (4096 pixels of 0xff203040);
  the TA writes no object (0 dwords) and all 64 regions are empty; 139 bytes of the parameter buffer
  and 17 of the TA data differ from the default word's, and the two dumps are byte for byte equal to
  each other, in the two runs.

**Not shown.** What each bit is for. Bit 12 stops the triangle before tiling, as the bits 11:9 do,
but the data say nothing of other primitives. Bit 18 hangs before the render completes, so its TA
output was not dumped. Bit 25 was cleared on one triangle only. Two runs of the same table.
