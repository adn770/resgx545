is not the result slot's initial fill (0xdeadc0de), so a stale read would have failed. One arm and
one job a T and pass, so no trend of the rate with T is claimed.

**Predictions.** Of the 8 rows frozen, 7 were evaluated. P33_T32, P33_T36, P33_T40, P33_T44 and
P33_T48 held in their first job and in their repeat (5 + 5 jobs), P33_REPEAT held (each repeat
equal to its first job in done, marker, r0, tick and gate). P33_TOP, "the register tested is
r123", states what the one blob does (it names no other register), so it could not fail and
shows nothing. P33_PAST (a register past the allocation) was not run by construction.

**What ran.** Ten arms with trc_hazard=1 (five T, two passes), each with one job, a reset to stock
and a gate. The arm of T = 32 failed in E540 without the hazard word and ran here with it. [infer]
That the hazard word is what let it arm rests on that difference alone: no arm without the word was
made here.

**Not shown.** Any register above r123 (it cannot be named), how many registers T = 32 to 48

in every lane, TRC 49 and above (not armed, the hang E540 predicted for 49 and 64 is neither
