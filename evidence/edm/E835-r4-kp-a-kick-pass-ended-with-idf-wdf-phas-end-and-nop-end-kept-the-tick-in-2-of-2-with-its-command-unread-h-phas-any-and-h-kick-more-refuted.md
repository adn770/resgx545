**What ran.** A plain tick pass of ours keeps the tick if and only if it executes the phase end
`phas #0, #0, pixel, end, parallel` before its `nop.end` (E817 held out, E830 replicated: 13 of 13
with it, 7 of 7 stopped without it), and E813's B3, a kick pass ended `idf, wdf, nop.end`, stopped
it (1 job). Q152 asked whether a kick pass needs the same phase end. KP is B3's kick pass with the
phase end: H-PHAS-ANY (any pass ended with the phase end is finished for the EDM: the tick is kept,
at least 4 ticks on the grid after the kick entry, the signature idle, and the kick's command stays
unread because the closed reader never runs) against H-KICK-MORE (a kick pass needs more: a stop
with E537's busy signature, nothing after the kick entry).

**What it shows.** A kick pass ended with the phase end keeps the tick, 2 of 2 (held out and
replicated), as a tick pass does: the phase end is what the EDM needs at the end of a pass of
either kind, and E813 B3's stop was its absence (H-PHAS-ANY consistent, H-KICK-MORE refuted). With
E817 and E830 this answers Q152 for tick and kick passes: the pass is finished for the EDM when
it executes `phas #0, #0, pixel, end, parallel` before its `nop.end`, whether or not the closed
task ran. [infer] The closed reader never ran, so the command stayed unread with no mark (read =
write - 1, dword 14 0, not E537's read = write + 1 and 0xad002600), and the EDM was idle with an
unread command; an unread command does not by itself make `CR_CLKGATESTATUS` read busy (E834's
unbumped kick did, with no event).

**What it does not show.** The slot 3 end-of-render pass ended the same way (stage e3p, E836);
other ends (`cfi.global` in a kick pass, the word order of the end); whether the unread command
would be read by the next kick, and what the kernel makes of a command left unread (each KP job
ended with a reset); a kick pass that our task logs without acting (KIND 2 acted on the first kick
pass only). P3 was not sent (no stop).
