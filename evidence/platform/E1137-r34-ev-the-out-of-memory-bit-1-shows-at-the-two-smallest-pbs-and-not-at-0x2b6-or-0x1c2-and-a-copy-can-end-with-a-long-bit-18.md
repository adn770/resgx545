**What it shows.** In this overflow form the out-of-memory event bit is DPM_OUT_OF_MEMORY_MT (bit
1), seen in every row at the two smallest PBs (0xe2, 0x82) and in none at 0x2b6 and 0x1c2 [HW, post
hoc: the split follows the PB size, the frozen per-row rule could not]; GBL (bit 2) shows only
in rows with bit 1, 31 us after it. REACHED_MEM_THRESH (bit 3) does not show at a sample of about 4
us. A copy can
end with a PIXELBE_END_RENDER level of 60 to 206 us, as a fill does (E547), in about two thirds of
copy rows on this module and tool; E547's ten copies (another tool and module) showed none.
CR_EVENT_STATUS read alone in a probe or copy window samples at 3.1 to 3.9 us, six to seven times
E1115's 541 ns [infer: the tool's CPU work shares the core with the sampler]; the T1 calibration
gave each later control its own window and every control passed.

bits within a job, and what a host write to CR_EVENT_HOST_CLEAR clears (Q111's host clause).
