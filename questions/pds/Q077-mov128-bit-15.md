Answered (E379): bit 15 (the field `lastpair` in the tables) makes mov128 write, to both halves of
its destination row, the ds0 pair the last mov32 or movs read from a data row (rows 0-23); row 4
was the pair of the template's last mov32 from a data row, ds0[9]. A movs to mr or douta from ds0[2]
or ds0[6] and a mov32 from ds0[2] name their pair; a movs from the temporary pair ds0[50], ds0[51], a
mov32 from ds1[9], a not of a temporary (ds0[48-55] or ds1[48-55]) and a mov64 from mr01 keep it;
bits 23:16, 9, 7, 1:0, the source row and swap change nothing (three runs of every case, two marker
sets). The data rows are read-only, so the pair named is its value. The simulator writes the pair
after those instructions and stops after any other (E400).

What is left: which other instructions or other rows and pairs (mov32 from ds0[3] to ds0[8], movs
from the pairs 4 and 8, movsa, a not of a data row, mov64 from mr23, mov16, the ALU, mul, the tests,
load and store, a plain mov128) set or keep the pair, and what it holds before any such read in a
program; what the bit is for.
