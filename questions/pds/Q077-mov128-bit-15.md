evidence = ["E259", "E261"]

With bit 15 set, mov128 into ds[52] from any of the 32 source rows, and into ds[48], ds[50], ds[52]
or ds[54] from rows 2 and 31 and from row 2 with swap, writes ds0[8] and ds0[9] (the data's row 4
ds0 pair, G and H) to the destination row's ds0 pair and to its ds1 pair (E259, E261). No form maps
the bit; the decoder ignores it, so the simulator stops on it.

What is open (pds_isa.md §7, item 14): whether row 4 is fixed or comes from another field of the
word (bits 23:16, 9, 7 and 1:0 were 0 in every case), and whether the bit selects another source
(a 64-bit pair repeated, a special such as mr) that happened to hold G and H.

To settle it: bit 15 with each of bits 23:16, 9, 7 and 1:0 also set, one at a time, the data rows
filled with distinct markers per dword and the temporaries complemented, and with mr written first.
